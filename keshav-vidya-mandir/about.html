Who We Are

Keshav Vidya Mandir Model School

केशव विद्या मंदिर मॉडल स्कूल

Keshav Vidya Mandir Model School (KVMM) is a private, co-educational CBSE day school at A-160, Vinod Nagar, Mandawali, East Delhi. Established in 1992, we nurture children from Nursery to Class 8 with English-medium instruction and a caring urban campus environment. Our motto: विद्या ददाति विनयम् — Knowledge Bestows Humility.

  • CBSE Affiliated · Nursery to Class 8
  • Private Co-Ed Day School · Urban Campus
  • English Medium · Session April to March
  • Library, Playground, Computer & Science Labs
  • Art, Dance, Music, Drama, Debate & Sports

Mission & Vision
