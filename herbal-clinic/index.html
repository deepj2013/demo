Who does what, and how little each person has to do.

This is the herbal half of the full IT solution: a public website, a private web application, booking and payment on WhatsApp and online, SEO, a Google listing, and WhatsApp marketing. Below is every person who uses it, and how small each task is. The medical judgement stays with the doctor.

What we build

Website so families can find you. Web application so every patient and plan is in one office. WhatsApp so they book, refill, and pay from the chat. SEO and Google listing so nearby search can reach the clinic. Marketing only to people who agreed.

What the clinic gives us

Photo, true bio, hours, address, fees, medicine names, a WhatsApp number, a Google account, and a payment account in the clinic’s name if people will pay online. You approve every public line.

How to read “easy”

They do this is a tap or a reply by a person.

The system does this happens with no extra work.

The doctor says yes is one reply. Medicine is not prepared before that.

No one installs an app. Booking does not need a password. The phone they already use for WhatsApp is enough.

The only menu they need to learn

They send Hi to the clinic number. The reply is five choices:

1 Book a visit
2 Refill my medicine
3 My next visit
4 Time and address
5 Talk to a person

Choice 4 is one reply. Choice 5 always reaches a human. The same menu can be in Hindi.

Patient · website

A new patient books from the website

Very easy · about 1 minute · 5 taps · no account needed

  1. They do this
    Open the clinic page

    From a search, a share, or your card. The page says what a visit is, why the plan is written, and how to start.

  2. They do this
    Tap Book a visit

    They choose new visit. Follow-up is the other choice, for people who already came.

  3. They do this
    Tap a free time

    Only open chairs are shown. Two people cannot take the same time.

  4. They do this
    Type name and phone

    That is the whole form for a first booking.

  5. They do this
    Tap Confirm

    The screen says the visit is held.

  6. The system does this
    Sends a WhatsApp confirmation

    Time, address, and what to bring. A reminder goes out again before the day. The front desk sees the name on today’s list.

They do not call to ask which hour is free. They do not make a password before the visit.

Patient · WhatsApp

A new patient books from the chat

Very easy · 4 or 5 replies · the chat they already know

  1. They do this
    Send Hi

    The menu arrives: 1 book, 2 refill, 3 next visit, 4 address, 5 a person.

  2. They do this
    Reply 1

    Then new visit or follow-up.

  3. The system does this
    Offers real free times

    They reply with the number of the time they want, then send their name.

  4. The system does this
    Confirms in the same chat

    The slot is now on the desk. A reminder follows before the day.

If they only wanted the address, they reply 4 and stop. One reply. If they are confused, they reply 5 and your helper answers.

Returning patient · helper · doctor

A refill, from the first message to collection

Patient: 3 replies. Doctor: 1 reply. Helper: 2 taps.

The patient does not retell the whole history. The WhatsApp number matches the file, so the last plan is already known. Nothing is prepared until the doctor agrees.

  1. Patient
    Hi, then 2, then हाँ

    The chat shows the last plan in plain words, for example “Tulsi blend, 30 days”. हाँ means “send the same request”. They can say no and stop.

  2. The system does this
    Parks the request

    Status becomes “asked”. The patient is told to wait. The morning note to the doctor includes “refill waiting”.

  3. Doctor says yes
    Reply YES 1, or tap Approve

    One reply on WhatsApp, or one tap on the desk. The doctor can change the plan or refuse. A refusal tells the patient to book a visit instead.

  4. The system does this
    Tells both sides

    The helper sees “approved”. The patient gets a short message. They are not asked to call.

  5. Helper
    Tap Ready, later tap Collected

    Ready sends “you can collect”. Collected closes the request. If a payment link is on, they can pay before they come. That link is an extra service.

Returning patient

The follow-up comes to them

Patient effort on the reminder day: one reply, if the suggested time is fine

  1. Doctor, at the visit
    Sets the next date before sending the plan

    One date field. It rides along with the medicine message.

  2. The system does this
    Sends a reminder before that date

    The message offers a free time and the choice to pick another. If they miss it, one second note can go out, only if you want that rule.

  3. Patient
    Replies yes, or picks another time

    Yes puts them on the roster. They can also reply 3 any day to ask “when is my next visit?”

  4. Helper, that morning
    Sees them on today’s list

    No separate follow-up notebook.

Family member · later module

A son or daughter books for a parent

Easy once this module is on · a few extra words in the same booking

  1. They do this
    Book as usual, on the site or on WhatsApp

    They add the patient’s name and phone, and their own phone as the contact.

  2. The system does this
    Keeps the medical file on the patient

    Reminders can go to the family number they gave. The parent’s old visits stay on one file, found by the patient’s phone.

Until you add this module, the helper can still book the parent from the desk in under a minute. The family member’s self-serve path is the later piece.

Anyone after hours

A message at night

The doctor’s effort: none until morning

  1. They do this
    Send Hi after closing

    Same word as in the daytime.

  2. The system does this
    Sends hours, address, and free morning times

    They can still finish a booking. The chat does not ring the doctor.

  3. Helper, next morning
    Sees the new name already on the list

    If they replied 5, that note is waiting as “a person asked”. It is a normal chat, not an alarm.

Front desk

The helper’s whole job is one list

Most actions are one tap. The list is open when the day starts.

A person at the door

  1. The system does this
    Today’s list is already there

    Also on the morning WhatsApp to the clinic. New, follow-up, and report look different.

  2. Helper
    Tap Arrived

    The doctor can see who is in the room.

  3. Helper
    Tap Done, or Did not come

    A no-show stays visible. The chair story is clear at the end of the day.

  4. Helper
    Enter the fee and tap Send receipt

    Visit fee, medicine fee, or both. The patient gets the receipt on WhatsApp.

Between visitors

  1. Helper
    Book a phone call the old way, if someone insists

    Pick the person, pick a free time. Same list. About a minute.

  2. Helper
    Tap Ready or Collected on a refill

    Only after the doctor has approved it. The button is not there before that.

  3. Helper
    Close a holiday in one step

    That day drops off the public booking page.

The helper does not delete a patient file, does not change a medicine, and does not approve a refill. Those stay with the doctor. If someone is upset or confused, the chat is already on the desk because they replied 5.

Doctor

The visit stays a conversation. The paperwork is short.

About one minute to send a plan, once your common plans are saved. A refill approval is one reply.

  1. The system does this
    Morning note

    How many visits, the first few names, and any refill waiting. You can ignore it and use the desk.

  2. Doctor
    Open the person marked Arrived

    Last visit, the old plan, and any “do not give” note are on top. You are not searching a notebook.

  3. Doctor
    Pick a saved plan, or type this person’s plan

    Name, morning or night, with food or without, number of days, food note, next date. The first time you save a common plan it takes a few minutes. After that you reuse it and still change a line for this person.

  4. Doctor
    Tap Send

    The patient receives those exact words on WhatsApp. The helper sees the same list to prepare. You do not dictate it a second time.

  5. Doctor says yes
    Refills: YES, change, or no

    From the phone or the desk. No is allowed. The patient is told either way.

You do not promise a cure on the website. You approve the public words once. After that, the page does not invent new claims. You do not have to answer the chat at night.
