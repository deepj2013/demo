Client · website

A new client books from the website

Easy · a few minutes · only because birth details take longer than a name

  1. They do this
    Open the practice page

    They see the services you switched on, what a session includes, and what they will receive. A chart, numerology, a match, vastu, or a service you named.

  2. They do this
    Tap the service, then a free time

    The length follows the service. A 30-minute numerology slot and a 60-minute match do not look the same. Taken times are hidden.

  3. They do this
    Type the details, or choose “I will send them on WhatsApp”

    Date, time, place, and the question. If the birth time is unknown, they say so. They are not blocked.

  4. They do this
    Tap Confirm

    If you take a fee before the session, a payment link can appear here. The payment company’s fee is extra, in your account.

  5. The system does this
    Confirms on WhatsApp and opens the file

    You see the question before the session. A reminder goes out before the hour. From day one you can write the report yourself and send it, even before a calculation service is connected.

They do not call to ask what they will get. That is written on the service. They do not sit in the session while someone hunts for the birth place, if they already sent it.

Client · WhatsApp

  2. They do this
    Pick a number for the service, then a number for the time

    Only free gaps are offered.

  3. The system does this
    Asks the details in small steps

  4. They do this
    Or they only wanted the fees

    Hi, then 4. One reply shows services and fees in your words. They can stop there.

Reply 5 reaches you or your assistant. The menu never ends in a dead end.

Client · then you

Birth details become a report, only after you send it

Client: answer the questions once. You: read, change any line, press send.

  1. Client
    Sends details on the site or by replying 2

    If they already booked, reply 2 adds the missing pieces to that same file.

  2. The system does this
    Marks the file “details in”

    Your day list shows who is ready and who is still missing a place or a question. If a calculation service is connected, chart figures and number totals can fill in. That service’s fee is extra. If it is not connected yet, you type those figures in the same boxes.

  3. The system does this
    Prepares a draft in your sections

    Chart points, numbers, timing notes, talking points. It is marked “not sent”. The client cannot open it. This draft can wait until you are ready. On day one, with no draft tool yet, you paste your own note into the same screen.

  4. You approve
    Edit, then tap Approve and send

    You rewrite anything you do not want. Name options stay as choices you tick. Nothing leaves until this tap.

  5. The system does this
    Delivers one finished note

    WhatsApp, and the client’s own page. They can ask for it again with Hi, then 3. They receive the note you approved, not the draft.

The session itself is still a conversation. Easy here means the file, the formatting, and the delivery. The judgement in the note is yours.

Returning client

The second session starts from the file

Very easy · one YES to reuse birth details

  1. They do this
    Hi, then 1

    The chat recognises the number.

  2. The system does this
    Offers the details already on file

    “We have your birth details from last time. Reply YES to use them.” They send only the new question, and a time.

  3. They do this
    Later, Hi then 3

    The last report you already sent opens again. They do not ask you to resend it by hand.

  4. The system does this
    Can remind them months later

    If you set a return month on the file. One date. The message goes out when that month comes.

Two people · match or family

One booking, two sets of details, one note

A little longer than one person · still one thread

  1. First person
    Books Match and sends their own details

    Same website or WhatsApp path. The service length is the longer one you set.

  2. First or second person
    Sends the second name, date, time, and place

    On the same chat, or the second person messages the clinic number and quotes the first person’s phone. The assistant can also type the second set in about a minute.

  3. The system does this
    Keeps both on one file

    A comparison draft can be prepared for you to edit. You add the final guidance. One send. Both can receive it if you choose both numbers.

You are not keeping a second notebook for the same house.

Anyone after hours

A message at night becomes a ready file by morning

Your effort at night: none

  1. They do this
    Send Hi after closing

    They can book, send details, or ask fees.

  2. The system does this
    Shares hours and still accepts a free slot or the details

    You are not expected to reply. Reply 5 waits as a normal note for the morning.

  3. You, next day
    Open a file that is already filled

    Question, time, and details are there before you speak.

Assistant

The assistant runs the calendar, not the reading

One tap for paid, arrived, or a manual booking

  1. The system does this
    Shows today, with who has paid and whose details are in

    The list is the job. There is no second diary.

  2. Assistant
    Books someone who called

    Service, free time, phone. About a minute. The confirmation still goes on WhatsApp.

  3. Assistant
    Marks the fee paid when the money arrives

    The day list drops the “fee open” mark. A receipt can go on WhatsApp.

  4. Assistant
    Answers a chat that asked for a person

    Reply 5 lands here. They can answer timings, fees, and “please send the birth place”. They do not press Approve and send.

The finished report stays on your button, unless you later choose to allow an assistant to send. The default is that only you send it.

Consultant

Your path on a session day

Admin around one session: open, edit, send. The talking is the real work.

  1. The system does this
    Morning list, and a note 30 minutes before

    Name, service, question. “Draft waiting” or “details missing”.

  2. You
    Open the draft and change any line

    If there is no draft yet, you write or paste the note on that same screen. The client still cannot see it.

  3. You
    Speak, with the question and old reports on the screen

    A repeat client’s last note is already there.

  4. You approve
    Tap Approve and send

    One finished document goes on WhatsApp. You can also keep it as a draft and send after the session.

  5. You or the assistant
    Glance at fees still open

    A short list in the evening. A payment link, if you use one, is sent from that row.
