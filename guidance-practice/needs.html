What is needed to make this business digital and useful

One list for the astrology, Vastu Shastra, and numerology practice. Build these pieces so clients can find you, book, send details, pay, and receive a report — while you keep the judgement and the final send.

Public face

So people can find you and trust you

  1. Public website with three doors
    • Home that names astrology, Vastu Shastra, and numerology clearly
    • One page (or door) per service you switch on
      • What a session includes
      • What the client must send (birth details, address, name spelling, etc.)
      • What they receive (chart note, Vastu note, numerology note)
      • Duration and fee in your words
    • About / photo / city so a stranger knows who they are booking
    • Book now path that leads to a real free slot
    • Hindi and English copy if you use both
  2. SEO and Google listing
    • Pages that match how people search (astrology / Vastu / numerology + your city)
    • Google Business Profile linked to the same hours, phone, and booking
    • Correct name, address, WhatsApp number, and website URL in one place

The three sciences

Digital tools for the work you already do

  1. Astrology workspace
    • Capture birth details: name, date, time, place, question
    • Allow “birth time unknown” without blocking the booking
    • Chart / figures area (typed by you at first, or filled by a calculation service later)
    • Draft report in your sections, marked “not sent”
    • You edit any line, then Approve and send as one finished note
    • Match / two-person file: two sets of details, one comparison draft, one send
  2. Vastu Shastra workspace
    • Address, facing, rooms, and optional photos
    • Written note you prepare and approve
    • Same booking, payment, and delivery path as the other services
  3. Numerology workspace
    • Date and name spelling captured cleanly
    • Name options you tick (client does not invent the list alone)
    • Number totals / draft you can edit before send

Calendar and money

Book a real gap, pay without phone calls

  1. Booking and calendar
    • Services you turn on/off (chart, numerology, match, Vastu, custom names)
    • Slot length follows the service (e.g. 30 vs 60 minutes)
    • Taken times hidden; only free gaps offered
    • Confirm by WhatsApp; reminder before the hour
    • Night messages still accept a free slot or details — no need for you to reply at once
    • Returning client: one YES to reuse birth details already on file
    • Optional return-month reminder months later
  2. Payment
    • Payment link on website confirm and on WhatsApp (if you take fee before session)
    • Payment account in your name; gateway fees extra
    • Mark paid (by assistant or auto); receipt can go on WhatsApp
    • Day list shows who still has fee open

One number · no new app

WhatsApp as the client’s only menu

  1. WhatsApp flow on your practice number
    • Client sends Hi — five choices:
      • 1 Book a session
      • 2 Send birth details
      • 3 My report
      • 4 Services and fees
      • 5 Talk to the consultant
    • Book path: pick service → pick free time → details one question at a time
    • Fees path: one reply in your words; they can stop there
    • Reply 5 always reaches you or the assistant (no dead end)
    • Same menu in Hindi and/or English
    • Deliver approved reports in the same chat; Hi then 3 reopens the last sent note
  2. WhatsApp marketing (only who agreed)
    • Consent stored on the client file
    • Festival / camp notes you approve once, then send only to agreed numbers
    • Message fees are extra

Private web application

One office for files, drafts, and the day

  1. Client file and day list
    • One file per person (or house for match): question, time, details, payment, reports
    • Status marks: details in / missing, draft waiting, fee open, report sent
    • Morning list: name, service, question, and what still needs you
    • Note ~30 minutes before a session
    • Month view: sessions, reports sent, drafts still with you, mix of the three services
  2. Report control (judgement stays yours)
    • System can prepare a draft when that module is on — client never sees it first
    • On day one, without a draft tool: you paste your own note into the same screen
    • Only you (by default) tap Approve and send
    • Client page + WhatsApp show only the finished note you approved
  3. Login only for staff
    • Password for you and the assistant
    • Clients who only book do not need an account — WhatsApp number is enough

Who uses it

Each person has a short path

  1. Client paths that must work
    • First session from the website
    • First session from WhatsApp (no form required)
    • Send birth details later (reply 2)
    • Returning client reuses old details
    • Two people / match on one file
    • Message after hours → complete file by morning
  2. Assistant (calendar, not the reading)
    • Open Today
    • Book a caller into a free gap (~1 minute)
    • Mark fee paid; send receipt if needed
    • Answer chats that asked for a person (reply 5)
    • Cannot Approve and send the report (unless you later allow it)
  3. Consultant (you)
    • Open next file; see question and old reports
    • Edit draft or write/paste the note
    • Speak in the session with the file on screen
    • Approve and send — one tap for delivery
    • Glance at open fees in the evening

Inputs from the business

What you must give so the system is real

You provide once · you approve every public line

  1. Practice facts
    • Which of the three you offer (and any extra service names)
    • Fees and session lengths per service
    • Hours and city
    • Photo and short bio you approve
    • Sample report style you like (so drafts match your voice)
    • WhatsApp number for the practice
    • Google account for the listing
    • Payment account in your name (if clients pay online)
  2. Ongoing rules you keep
    • You approve every public website line before it goes live
    • You press send on every report — software formats and delivers; judgement is yours
    • Marketing only to people who agreed

Useful means: no second diary, no hunting for birth place in the session, one WhatsApp menu, reports that leave only when you say so, and new people who can find you on Google and book without calling.
