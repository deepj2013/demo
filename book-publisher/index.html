The Problem

Why publishers need a unified platform

Traditional publishing houses juggle spreadsheets, WhatsApp, separate Amazon seller panels, manual royalty calculations, and disconnected author communication. Independent authors expect transparency. Readers expect to buy anywhere.

Why — The Pain

Publishers lose time and trust when systems don't talk to each other.

  • Orders scattered across Amazon, Flipkart, website, Kindle
  • Authors calling daily for sales updates
  • ISBN & copyright tracked in emails and folders
  • Manuscript versions lost in WhatsApp
  • No professional storefront for direct sales
  • Royalty calculations done manually every quarter
  • No bulk WhatsApp or social media for book promotion
  • No standard process — every book handled differently

What — The Solution

Bookspot Platform is a white-label publishing operating system — publish, promote, and operate on autopilot.

  • Publisher Command Center — full business control
  • Author Self-Service Portal — register, upload, track
  • Public Bookstore Website — shop, discover, submit manuscript
  • Bulk WhatsApp Marketing — boost sales & attract authors
  • Social Media Management — Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn
  • Standard Business SOPs — 5 pre-built operational workflows
  • 12 Automations — orders, royalties, launches, notifications
  • Marketplace Hub — Amazon, Flipkart, Kindle sync

How — The Flow

From manuscript to marketplace in one seamless pipeline.

  • Author registers on your branded portal
  • Uploads manuscript + metadata + cover brief
  • Publisher reviews → editing → design → ISBN
  • Book goes live on website + marketplaces
  • Orders sync automatically to both dashboards
  • Auto WhatsApp + social launch campaign triggers
  • Royalties calculated and paid on schedule
