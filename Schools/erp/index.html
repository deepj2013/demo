Complete Feature List

Every module your school needs — from admission to alumni management

👨‍🎓

Student Information System (SIS)

  • Online admission & enrollment with document upload
  • Student profile with photo, medical records & emergency contacts
  • Class & section allocation with roll number management
  • Student ID card generation (print & digital)
  • Transfer certificate (TC) & bonafide certificate generation
  • Alumni database & tracking
  • Bulk import/export via Excel
  • Student promotion & demotion workflow
📅

Attendance Management

  • Daily student attendance (manual, biometric, RFID)
  • Teacher & staff attendance tracking
  • Subject-wise attendance for senior classes
  • Auto SMS/email alerts to parents for absentees
  • Monthly attendance reports & defaulter lists
  • Leave application & approval workflow
  • Holiday & event calendar integration
  • Attendance analytics dashboard
📝

Examination & Results

  • Exam schedule creation (unit tests, half-yearly, annual)
  • Marks entry by teachers with validation
  • Grade calculation as per CBSE/state board norms
  • Report card generation (PDF) with co-scholastic grades
  • Online result publishing for parents
  • Rank lists, toppers & performance analytics
  • Question paper management
  • Remedial class tracking for weak students
💰

Fee & Finance Management

  • Fee structure setup (class-wise, category-wise)
  • Online fee payment (UPI, cards, net banking)
  • Fee receipt generation & auto SMS confirmation
  • Due fee reminders & defaulter reports
  • Concession & scholarship management
  • Expense tracking & budget management
  • Payroll integration for staff salaries
  • Financial reports (daily, monthly, annual)
👩‍🏫

Staff & HR Management

  • Teacher & staff profiles with qualifications
  • Class & subject assignment
  • Leave management (CL, EL, medical leave)
  • Salary processing & payslip generation
  • Performance appraisal & feedback system
  • Recruitment & onboarding workflow
  • Staff attendance with biometric integration
  • Document vault (certificates, contracts)
📚

Academic Management

  • Curriculum & syllabus management (CBSE/ICSE)
  • Time table creation with conflict detection
  • Lesson plan & homework assignment
  • Online class scheduling (Google Meet/Zoom integration)
  • Study material upload (PDF, video, links)
  • Assignment submission & grading
  • Academic calendar with term dates
  • Class diary & daily report for parents
📢

Communication & Notices

  • Circular & notice board (class/section-wise)
  • Bulk SMS to parents & staff
  • Push notifications via mobile app
  • Email campaigns & newsletters
  • Parent-teacher meeting (PTM) scheduling
  • Event invitations & RSVP tracking
  • WhatsApp integration for quick updates
  • Emergency broadcast alerts
🚌

Transport Management

  • Route & stop management with GPS mapping
  • Vehicle & driver assignment
  • Student transport fee linking
  • Live bus tracking for parents (GPS)
  • Transport attendance
  • Fuel & maintenance expense tracking
  • Route optimization
  • Transport fee reports
📖

Library Management

  • Book catalog with ISBN & barcode
  • Issue/return tracking with due dates
  • Fine calculation for overdue books
  • Digital library & e-book access
  • Reading history per student
  • Stock & inventory management
  • Popular books & recommendation engine
  • Library usage reports
🏠

Hostel Management

  • Room allocation & bed management
  • Hostel fee & mess charges
  • Visitor log & gate pass system
  • Leave & outing permission workflow
  • Mess menu & attendance
  • Discipline & incident reporting
  • Medical records for boarders
  • Hostel inventory management
📊

Reports & Analytics

  • Custom report builder with filters
  • Dashboard with real-time KPIs
  • Attendance trends & heatmaps
  • Fee collection analytics
  • Academic performance comparisons
  • Export to PDF, Excel, CSV
  • Scheduled report emails
  • Board compliance reports (UDISE, etc.)
📱

Parent & Student Mobile App

  • View attendance, marks & report cards
  • Pay fees online with payment history
  • Receive notices & circulars
  • Homework & assignment tracking
  • Bus tracking in real-time
  • Apply for leave online
  • Chat with class teacher
  • Event calendar & photo gallery
