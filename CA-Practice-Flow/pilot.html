Pilot — do not migrate all 400 first

30–50 clients. Prove the model. Then batch scale.

Parallel run with Excel for one full cycle. Success is measured — not assumed.

Recommended pilot mix

  • ~15 GST + Bookkeeping
  • ~10 GST + TDS
  • ~10 ITR (individual / firm)
  • ~5–10 Audit / Registration / DSC

Selection criteria

  • WhatsApp-reachable primary contact
  • Tally data reasonably clean
  • Mix of simple + one complex each category
  • Client willing to try portal uploads
  • Not in active notice/litigation (initially)
