About our school

Nurturing Young Minds in the Heart of Delhi

Sunrise India Public School is a general education school in West Vinod Nagar, Mandawali. We believe learning should feel bright, safe, and joyful — just like a sunrise.

  • Holistic development — academics, arts & sports
  • Safe, colourful classrooms and play areas
  • Parent partnership and transparent communication
  • Convenient Mandawali / West Vinod Nagar location
