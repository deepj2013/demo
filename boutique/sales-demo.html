What boutique owners struggle with today

Excel, WhatsApp, and generic retail software were never made for design → stitch → stock → sell.

Hidden losses

No real garment cost

Fabric, thread, buttons, waste, and tailor wages live in different notebooks. Selling price is a guess — margin disappears.

Chaos

Stock without locations

“Where is that burgundy silk?” No rack map, no photos, no in/out trail. Reorders happen too late or too early.

Disconnected

Shop, CRM & store siloed

Online orders, walk-in clients, and vendors don’t talk. Reports are rebuilt every month by hand.
